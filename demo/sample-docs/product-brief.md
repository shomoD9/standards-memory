# Acme Notes: product brief

Acme Notes is a small note-taking app for teams. Notes are plain Markdown, shared in workspaces, and searchable across every workspace a person belongs to.

## Problem

Teams keep meeting notes in personal documents, so decisions get lost. People spend time asking "where did we write that down?" instead of doing the work.

## Proposal

Ship a shared workspace with three features in the first release:

1. Markdown notes with live preview.
2. Workspace-wide search with filters for author and date.
3. A "decisions" tag that collects tagged lines into one page per workspace.

## Success measures

- 60% of active workspaces create at least one decisions page in the first month.
- Median search-to-open time under 10 seconds.

## Risks

- Search quality on large workspaces is unproven.
- Teams may not adopt the decisions tag without a template.

## Next step

Decision needed from the product lead: approve the three-feature scope so engineering can start the first sprint.
