# Review of the Acme Notes product brief

The brief is clear and short. The problem statement is concrete, and the three-feature scope is realistic for a first release.

## Strengths

- Success measures are numeric and checkable.
- The decisions tag is a distinctive idea that ties directly to the stated problem.

## Concerns

- The 60% adoption target has no baseline. It is reportedly typical for team tools, but that claim is unsupported here.
- Search is the riskiest feature and gets one line. It needs a plan for large workspaces, for example a prototype script that indexes 100,000 notes and reports query latency.
- No mention of how notes are exported, which enterprise buyers will ask about.

## Recommendation

Approve the scope with two conditions: add a baseline for the adoption target, and run the search prototype before the second sprint. The next action is for the brief's author to revise the document and resubmit.
